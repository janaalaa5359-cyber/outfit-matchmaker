What does this app do?

"What should I wear?" is the daily question every girl knows, and choosing an outfit can take a long time, especially before a wedding, a presentation or a family gathering. Made by girls, for girls, Outfit Matchmaker collects a few details about the occasion and the main piece of clothing, then applies colour-harmony and dress-code rules to recommend a complete outfit with a match score.

The form is now sent to a Flask server, checked on the server, scored by a trained machine learning model and saved in a SQLite database.

How It Works

  1. Occasion sets the dress code

    Weddings call for heels and a clutch, work for structured pieces, sport for trainers and a backpack.

  2. Colour harmony picks the partners

    Each main colour has neutral and complementary partners. Neutrals (black, white, beige, navy, grey) pair with almost anything.

  3. Style adjusts the accents

    Minimal keeps one neutral tone, bold adds a contrasting accent, classic leans on gold and leather.

  4. Budget and season refine the result

    The budget decides the price tier of missing pieces; the season chooses fabrics such as linen or wool.
