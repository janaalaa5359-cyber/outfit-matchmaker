About the project

Why Outfit Matchmaker?

From a single piece of clothing to a complete, matching look.

Problem Statement

"What should I wear?" is the question almost every girl asks herself before going out. The wardrobe is full, yet nothing seems to match: which shoes go with this burgundy dress? Which bag suits a navy outfit for a presentation? Is this colour right for a wedding?

Choosing an outfit often takes a long time: trying on piece after piece, asking sisters and friends for their opinion, and still feeling unsure. This daily stress makes girls late, repeat the same safe looks again and again, or buy new pieces they don't really need.

Existing fashion apps are mostly online shops. They try to sell new items instead of helping a girl make the most of what she already owns, and they rarely consider the occasion, local dress codes such as the abaya, and her budget together.

💗 From girls, to girls: we are six female students who face this problem every day ourselves. Outfit Matchmaker is the friend we wished we had: it understands the occasion, respects our style and our culture, and helps us decide with confidence in seconds instead of minutes.

Target Users

Our main users are girls and young women who want to look good without wasting time:

  • University students choosing outfits for lectures, presentations and graduation.
  • Working women who need quick, elegant and appropriate looks for the office.
  • Girls getting ready for weddings, Eid visits, family gatherings and outings with friends.
  • Anyone who wants to style the abaya or a single favourite piece in new ways.
  • Women's fashion stores that want to offer "complete the look" suggestions.

Project Objective

Save girls time and stress before every occasion. We aim to build a web application that receives details about an occasion and a main clothing piece, and recommends a coordinated outfit (bottom piece, shoes, bag and accent colour) with a match score. The long-term objective is a data-driven recommender trained on rated outfit combinations.

Expected Output

A Result card showing:

  • An illustrated preview of the full outfit that redraws live when the style changes (classic adds a belt, modern a geometric panel, bold a striped piece, minimal stays plain).
  • A colour palette (main, partner and accent colours).
  • The suggested bottom or layering piece, shoes, bag and optional accessory.
  • A match score (0–100%) predicted by a trained machine learning model, with a short explanation.
  • An estimated price tier based on the budget.

Each valid look is saved in a SQLite database and listed on the Saved Looks page.

Homework 2: What Changed

  • Flask: the form is submitted with POST to a Flask route, checked on the server, and the pages are rendered with Jinja templates.
  • Persistent database: looks are stored in SQLite with Flask-SQLAlchemy and stay saved after the server restarts.
  • Real ML model: a scikit-learn model predicts the match score, replacing the Homework 1 simulated score. It is also available as a JSON API (POST /predict).
